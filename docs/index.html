The marketing team of your
unmanned AI company. 24/7.

Eight AI teammates: one answers comments every 10 minutes, one listens to what people say every morning, one reads your numbers, two get every upload out, inside Ego Lite with your own logins. Your agent writes the words; every post waits for your approval.

The team

comment-replier

Comment replier

Collects new Instagram comments and posts the replies your agent wrote, in each commenter's language, 15 to 30 seconds apart.

Gate: questions needing facts go to you
listener

Listener

Searches X and Threads for your keywords, keeps only real mentions, ranks them by reach and writes a report.

Read only: never likes or replies
studio-finisher

Upload finisher

Fills title, description, playlists and tags from your sheet, saves, then reloads and checks what is really saved.

Never trusts "Changes saved"
crossposter

Crossposter

One video into posts for X, Threads and LinkedIn. Checks X weighted length, queues drafts, types on approve.

Final Post click is yours by default
benchmarker

Benchmarker

Before an upload: the top recent YouTube results for your keyword with views, upload age, length and views a day, plus title patterns.

Read only
analyst

Analyst

Reads your own public X and YouTube numbers every morning into a daily CSV; the desk shows the change since last time.

Read only
breakdown

Breakdown

One post that took off: its text, numbers and frames from the first seconds, for your agent to study.

Read only
form-filler

Form filler

Reads a creator-program form, fills it from your profile, and stops before Submit with a screenshot.

Submit is always yours
tagger

Tagger

Shopping tags on every video that fits.

Rules the team keeps

Your logins stay yours. The kit never types passwords, codes or card numbers.
Approve to send. Posts and replies wait in the inbox unless you trust a role with autoSend.
Human pace. Replies are spaced out; listening never likes, follows or replies.
Stop, do not guess. If a site changed and a button is missing, the role stops and says so.
